# Moelomol

A 2D farming and adventure RPG made in Godot 4.7.2 for Windows, macOS and Linux.

You are the last one left after a calamity. You farm crops and animals at your
single base, which is a peaceful place. Beyond it, you explore, forage and fight.
You craft and enhance equipment from what you grow and what monsters drop. You
never meet another character: you piece together what happened to the world from
the places you walk through and the notes left behind.

## Status

Milestone 0 (foundation). There is no Godot project and no gameplay yet. The
repository currently holds the project's decisions and design documents.
