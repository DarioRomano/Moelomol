# Playtest: magic and status effects

**For:** the project lead. **Why:** the rules are tested automatically, but
whether magic feels like "prepare, keep away, then pay off" (readable
stacks, a satisfying Release, Wardstep as escape and attack) can only be
judged by playing.

## 1. Build

The PR or release build, arena as before. Press **F7** until the HUD's
second weapon is magic (greatsword → bow → magic), then **Tab / RB**. A
violet Focus bar appears under stamina while magic is in hand. Coloured pips
above a creature show its stacks: ember = Smoulder, blue = Chill, violet =
Rot, up to five each.

| Magic | Keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Ember bolt (Smoulder) | J | X / Square |
| Frost shard (Chill) | tap K | tap RT / R2 |
| Rot pool | hold K | hold RT / R2 |
| Release | L | Y / Triangle |
| Wardstep (dodge) | Space | A / Cross |

## 2. Checks

Write a short note for each: fine / problem (what).

2. **Tap vs hold:** a tap of heavy is a Frost shard, holding 0.25 s places a
   Rot pool (a ring grows where it will land). Easy to get the one you
   mean? Result:
3. **Full stacks:** 5 Smoulder spreads to a neighbour; 5 Chill freezes
   (pale blue, stars); 5 Rot halves the shelled creature's shell. Do you
   notice each when it happens? Result:
4. **Chill slow:** chilled creatures walk and telegraph slower. Noticeable,
   useful? Result:
5. **Release:** load a creature with all three effects, step in, Release.
   Does it feel like the biggest payoff, and worth the risk of stepping
   close (28 px cone)? 5/5/5 does 360 damage: too much? Result:
6. **Combinations:** Shatter (Smoulder + Chill) hits creatures around the
   target; Blight bloom (Smoulder + Rot) spreads Rot; Brittle (Chill + Rot)
   staggers. Can you tell which happened? Result:
7. **Casting while moving:** casts slow you to 60% but never root you;
   getting hit during a cast's windup cancels it. Fair? Result:
8. **Focus:** do you run out? Does the faster refill after 3 s unhit reward
   clean kiting? Result:
9. **Wardstep:** the dodge becomes a 40 px blink that leaves a Chill pool.
