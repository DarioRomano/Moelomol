# Playtest: farming and the day–night cycle

**For:** the project lead. **Why:** the rules are tested automatically, but
the pace of the day, how the light feels, and whether farming is pleasant
can only be judged by playing. Updated 2026-10-10 for the lead's decisions
on Q21–Q27: a 20-minute day that cycles on its own, farming by spells paid
in Mana, and the watering spell with its tiers up to rain.

## 1. Build

The PR or release build. From the title press **F2** until the farm appears
(title → test card → renderer features → arena → farm), or launch with
`-- --start-scene farm_test`. Keyboard once, controller once.

| | Keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D | Left stick |
| Cast the selected spell (Till, Water, Sow catmint, Sow radish) | J | X / Square |
| Interact (harvest a ripe crop; the farmhouse door: sleep) | Space | A / Cross |
| Next / previous spell | E / Q | RB / LB |

Developer keys: **F8** sleep now, **F9** time ×30, **F10** next watering
tier (Water, Water II, Water III, Rain; the upgrades that unlock them are
not built), **F3** performance overlay. The top-right shows the day, time,
phase and rain. The top-left shows the Mana bar, the selected spell with its
Mana cost, and what you carry.

## 2. Checks

Write a short note for each: fine / problem (what).

1. **Targeting:** the outlined tile in front of you is the one you cast on;
   for Water the whole area is outlined. Easy to aim, including diagonals,
   with keys and with the stick? Result:
2. **The farming loop:** Till a few tiles, Water them, Sow catmint and
   radish. Sleep at the door. Next morning, are they a stage further? Do
   watered and dry soil read clearly? Result:
3. **Mana:** Till 2, Water 4 (tier 1), Sow 1, out of 100, refilled only by
   sleep. How far does a day's Mana go? Do you run out too soon, or never?
   Result:
4. **The watering spell:** 4 tiles at first (2 × 2, ahead and to your
   right). Is the shape easy to predict and aim? With F10, try Water II
   (3 × 3), Water III (5 × 5) and Rain (the whole farm until 6:00). Do they
   feel like upgrades worth working towards? Result:
5. **Growth by night:** radish ripens after 3 watered nights, catmint after
   4. Satisfying, or too slow? Result:
6. **The day's pace:** one full cycle is 20 minutes, and it keeps going if
   you never sleep. The day count and growth happen at 6:00 either way. Is
   that clear while playing? Result:
7. **Light:** watch dusk, night and dawn (F9 helps). Windows light up at
   dusk and go out at dawn. Does dusk feel like the emotional peak? Is
   night dark but readable? Does rain read as rain? Result:
8. **Casting:** a short glow at the hands while casting (warm green and
   gold; Q28 asks whether it should ever show violet). Readable? Result:
9. **Rumble:** soft pulses per spell, a long soft roll for Rain, a dull
   buzz when a spell does nothing or Mana runs out. Pleasant, or noise?
   Result:
