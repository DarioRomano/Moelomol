# Playtest: hammer, armour and weapon swap

**For:** the project lead. **Why:** the hammer's rules are tested
automatically, but whether its timing *feels* fair (the sweet spot, the
charge, the weight of a perfect strike, the rumble) can only be judged by
playing.

## 1. Build

The PR or release build, arena as before (F2 from the title until the arena,
or `-- --start-scene combat_arena`). Keyboard once, controller once. You start
with the greatsword; **Tab / RB** swaps to the hammer (1.5 s before the next
swap). The top-left line shows the weapon, what you are doing, Rhythm and the
charge time.

| Hammer | Keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Jab | J | X / Square |
| Charge (hold), strike (release) | hold K | hold RT / R2 |
| Ground stamp | L | Y / Triangle |
| Swap weapon | Tab | RB / R1 |

The bottom-right creature has a **stone shell** (grey band and a grey bar
above its health): 40% of damage gets through until the shell breaks.

## 2. Checks

Write a short note for each: fine / problem (what).

1. **Finding the sweet spot (F6 on):** hold heavy; after 0.9 s the hammer head
   flashes white and the meter above you enters its marked zone; release
   then for a perfect strike (shockwave ring, everything near staggered).
   Is the 150 ms window learnable? Too tight, too loose? Result:
2. **Signals:** is the flash enough to time by eye? With a controller, can
   you time it by the rumble click alone, eyes off the character? (There is
   no sound yet.) Result:
3. **Trigger:** on RT / R2, does releasing feel precise, or does the trigger
   deadzone (0.3) make the release land late? Result:
4. **Rhythm:** chain perfect strikes; the window narrows each time (down to
   75 ms at 3). Does Rhythm 3 feel like a skill ceiling or a wall? Result:
5. **Early and Overstrain:** a tap gives a weak strike; holding too long
   turns the hammer red and drains stamina until it comes down by itself.
   Does Overstrain read as a mistake, and is the drain harsh enough that
   holding forever is never safe? Result:
6. **Walking while charging:** is 35% speed useful for positioning, or too
   slow / too fast? Result:
7. **Perfect strike weight:** with creatures active, does the perfect
   strike (120 ms hit-stop, shockwave, heaviest rumble) feel like the
   biggest hit in the game? Result:
8. **Armour:** chip the shelled creature with the greatsword, then try a
   perfect hammer strike on a fresh shell. Does the shatter feel worth the
   timing? Is 60 shell points right? Result:
9. **Jabs and Ground stamp:** do jabs interrupt a creature's approach? Does
   Ground stamp get a creature off you when it is too close to charge? Can
   you cancel a charge with stamp or dodge when a lunge comes? Result:
10. **Swap:** swapping mid-fight feels like a decision, not spam; 1.5 s
    cooldown right? Swapping out of an attack's recovery okay? Result:
11. **Rumble:** charge hum, sweet-spot click, hammer hits, perfect strike.
    Distinct? Too strong? Result:
12. **Wide sweet spot (accessibility):** add
    ```
    [accessibility]
    hammer_wide_sweet_spot=true
    ```
    to `settings.cfg` in the game's user folder (Windows
    `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Moelomol`, Linux
    `~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/Moelomol`), restart: the window is
    300 ms and never narrows. Comfortable? Result:
