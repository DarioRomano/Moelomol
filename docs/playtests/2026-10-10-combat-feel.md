# Playtest: combat feel changes

**For:** the project lead. **Why:** these are your five changes after
playing the arena. The rules are tested, but whether they now *feel* right
can only be judged by playing. Use the PR build; same arena, same keys.

## Checks

Write a short note for each: fine / problem (what).

1. **Stagger resets the attack:** stagger a creature during its red
   telegraph (a cleave, a shove into a pillar, a perfect hammer strike, or
   Frost to five Chill). When it recovers, does it pause briefly and then
   start a fresh telegraph, rather than seeming to finish the old attack?
   Result:
2. **Greatsword and hammer reach (+40%):** do swings now connect where you
   expect them to? Does anything feel too long, so that you hit creatures
   you thought were out of reach? Result:
3. **Hammer charge rumble (controller):** hold heavy with the hammer. It
   should rise through levels 1 and 2 with a click at each, hum steadily
   with a sharper click at level 3, and turn rough with a jolt when
   overcharged. Can you time a perfect strike by feel alone? Too strong,
   too weak? Result:
4. **Other new rumble:** dodge, a lunge passing through your dodge, Brace
   taking a hit, a shell breaking, swap, running out of stamina, the bow's
   draw tension, magic's hold. Any that is too much, or missing? Result:
5. **Three charge levels:** three pips above the meter light at 0.3, 0.6
   and 0.9 s. Do levels 1 and 2 feel useful as quicker, weaker strikes, or
   only as steps on the way to level 3? Result:
6. **Cone shockwave:** a perfect level-3 strike sends a 70° cone forward,
