# Playtest: Millbrook's objective, notes and satchel

**For:** the project lead. **Why:** the rules are tested, but whether the
objective can be found without a quest log (Q37), and how reading notes
feels, can only be judged by playing.

**Build:** the PR build of `millbrook-objective-and-notes`. Millbrook from
the title (F2) or `--start-scene millbrook`. Interact: F / B. Satchel:
I / View.

**Setup:** machine, screen, controller or keyboard.

## Checks

Write a short note for each: fine / problem (what).

1. **Finding the objective without help:** starting fresh, do you notice
   the road running into the floodwater at the signpost, find the mill,
   read the miller's note, and work out where the crank is? How long did
   it take? Where did you hesitate? Result:
2. **The notes:** do they read as people from before, in their own voices?
   Any that explain too much, or too little? Do the lost-pet poster and the
   child's drawing (Pip the cat) feel ordinary, as stage 1 should? Result:
3. **The note paper:** easy to read on your screen? Is the red loose
   thread on the miller's note noticeable without being loud? Result:
4. **The satchel:** is re-reading easy (up/down, F / B, I / View)? Does
   newest first feel right? Result:
5. **The buttons:** F / B to interact and I / View for the satchel: do they
   feel natural next to the combat buttons, on keyboard and controller?
   Result:
6. **The crank and Grist:** taking the crank from the hook in Grist's yard,
   by day (sneaking or fighting) and by night (Grist asleep): does it work
   as a choice? Result:
7. **The sluice and the drain:** is opening the sluice and watching the
   meadow drain over ten seconds a satisfying moment? Too slow, too fast?
   Result:
8. **Grist's heart:** after the fight, is the heart easy to spot and take?
   Did you find the miller's note about Grist that appears afterwards?
   Result:
