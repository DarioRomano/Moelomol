# Playtest: Millbrook's creatures and Grist

**For:** the project lead. **Why:** the rules are tested and the review
sheets show each telegraph, but whether each creature is fun to fight, and
whether its one idea comes across, can only be judged by playing.

**Build:** the PR build of `creature-kinds-and-grist`. Open the combat arena
(F2 from the title). **F11** cycles the creature sets; **F12** toggles
night; F6 makes creatures passive; F7 changes the second weapon.

**Setup:** machine, screen, controller or keyboard.

## Checks

Write a short note for each: fine / problem (what).

1. **Field mice:** do they feel like a swarm (zigzagging in from
   different sides, scattering when hit)? Is a pack of five fair with the
   greatsword? Too fast at 70 px/s? Result:
2. **Rooks:** can you read the dive's lane in time (0.5 s)? Does waiting
   for the landing feel like a puzzle or a chore? With the bow, does
   shooting them out of the air feel good? Result:
3. **Hedgehogs:** is it clear that light hits barely hurt a curled one, and
   that heavy hits and the hammer open it? Does the roll into a wall (the
   daze) feel like a reward for dodging? Result:
4. **Flood toads:** is the ground circle easy to see and fair? Is the slick
   (40% slower for 3 s) annoying in a good way or a bad way? Result:
5. **Stooks:** among the sheaves, is the reveal a surprise but not a cheap
   shot? Is 24 px close enough to feel like an ambush? Result:
6. **Moths (press F12 if needed):** do they read as night creatures drawn
   to the light? Are the darts too many at once? Result:
7. **Grist, phase one:** does the charge's lane give you time to sidestep
   (0.8 s)? Is the stun against walls and millstones a satisfying
   opening? Do the burst sacks and mice make you steer its charges? Result:
8. **Grist, phase two:** is the wallow clear (it cannot be hurt in the
   mud)? Does the mud armour make you reach for the hammer? Is the double
   charge readable? Is the rolling millstone, which comes back after its
   bounce, fair? Result:
9. **Grist at night (F12):** does creeping past the sleeping boar feel
   possible? Is the wake distance (24 px) fair? Result:
10. **Overall:** is Grist's 600 health about right: a fight of a few
    minutes, not ten? Result:

Notes per check; tuning changes come back as a PR with the new values for
