# Playtest: bow

**For:** the project lead. **Why:** the bow's rules are tested
automatically, but whether it *flows* (moving while drawing, the dodge
shot, keeping Flow up, reading arrows in flight, the rumble) can only be
judged by playing.

## 1. Build

The PR or release build, arena as before. You start with the greatsword and
the hammer: press **F7** once to put the bow in the second slot, then
**Tab / RB** to take it in hand. The top-left line shows Flow, the draw
stage and the Volley mark's time left.

| Bow | Keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Quick shot | J | X / Square |
| Draw (hold), loose (release) | hold K | hold RT / R2 |
| Dodge shot | K during a roll (Space) | RT during a roll (A) |
| Volley: mark, then rain | L, then L again within 4 s | Y, then Y again |
| Lock on | Left Shift | LT / L2 |

## 2. Checks

Write a short note for each: fine / problem (what).

1. **Arrows in flight:** can you follow an arrow and see what it hits? Is
   360 px/s fast enough to feel like a bow, slow enough to read? Result:
2. **Draw stages:** three pips above you light at 0.3 / 0.7 / 1.2 s, each
   with a rumble tick. Does each stage feel distinct (strong, piercing
   through a line, heavy knockback)? Result:
3. **Clean release:** the newest pip flashes white for 80 ms after a stage;
   releasing then adds 25%. Noticeable? Learnable? Result:
4. **Moving while drawing** at 60% speed: does it feel like kiting, or
   sluggish? Result:
5. **Stage 3 hold:** costs stamina every second, and the arrow flies by
   itself when stamina runs out. Fair? Result:
6. **Dodge shot:** roll (towards or past a creature) and press heavy during
   the roll; a piercing arrow flies as the roll ends. Does it feel like the
   bow's signature move? Is the timing forgiving enough? Result:
7. **Flow:** hits build it to 5 (drawing up to 40% faster); one hit taken
   loses it all. Does holding Flow feel rewarding? Is the loss too harsh?
   Result:
8. **Volley:** mark a creature (red ring), then call the rain; it falls
   after 0.4 s in three waves, wider with Flow spent. Do the waves read?
   Does spending Flow on a wider rain feel like a real choice? Result:
9. **Soft aim and lock-on:** without lock-on, shots snap to a creature
   within about 30° of your facing. Fair with eight keyboard directions?
   With lock-on, do you hit what you mean to? Result:
10. **Rumble:** stage ticks, release snap, arrow hits, heavy-arrow hit.
    Distinct? Too strong? Result:
11. **Swapping with F7 / Tab:** greatsword + bow, hammer + bow. Any
    combination that feels wrong? Result:
12. **Overlay (F3)** while shooting at all three creatures: FPS and logic
    ms. Result:
