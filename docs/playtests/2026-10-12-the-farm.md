# Playtest: the farm, and the way down to Millbrook

**For:** the project lead. **Why:** the layout and the join to Millbrook are
tested, but how the homestead feels to walk, and how the gate and the fade
feel, can only be judged by playing.

**Build:** the PR build of `farm-homestead`. The farm from F2 or
`--start-scene farm`. Farming as before (J / X cast, Space / A interact, Q E
/ LB RB spells; the farmhouse door is the bed).

**Setup:** machine, screen, controller or keyboard.

## Checks

Write a short note for each: fine / problem (what).

1. **The homestead:** does it read as one warm, lived-in place inside its
   hedge? Can you tell the hedge, the thicket beyond it and the woods
   apart? Result:
2. **Walking it:** is it the right size for one person (house to field,
   field to pond, pond to gate)? Anything you bump into that you should
   not, or walk through that you should not? Result:
3. **The first field:** is it clear which ground can be tilled (the field
   with its earth edge) and which cannot yet (the old fields under long
   grass)? Result:
4. **Wide screens (21:9, 32:9):** does seeing the thicket beyond the hedges
   give the sense of a bigger farm still to win back? Result:
5. **Down to Millbrook and back:** out through the south gate and down the
   lane, then up Millbrook's lane home. Does the 0.3 s fade feel right?
   Does arriving facing away from the gate feel natural? Result:
6. **Things remembered:** read a note, take the crank, go home and come
   back. All as you left it? Crops watered before a trip: grown once on
   your return? Result:
7. **Falling:** fall in Millbrook. Waking at the farm at 6:00 in front of
   the door (the bed and the cat come with the house's inside): acceptable
   for now? Result:
8. **Dusk at the farm:** windows and the porch lamp coming on. Result:
