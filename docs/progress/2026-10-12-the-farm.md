# 2026-10-12: The farm, joined to Millbrook

The lead asked for a title screen that drops the player at the farm or the
last save, and for saving. The farm in the accepted layout was not built
yet, so the lead chose (2026-10-12) to **build the real farm first**, to
save **when sleeping and at the gate either way**, and to keep **one save**.
This note covers the first of two pull requests: the farm and the join to
Millbrook. The title screen and saving follow in the second.

## Done

- **The farm from the accepted layout** (`base.md`): 160 x 96 tiles.
  - `FarmLayout` holds the estate map (4 x 4 tiles per character) and the
    homestead map (2 x 2) exactly as in the design doc; tests compare them
    with `docs/design/images/estate.txt` and `base.txt`.
  - `FarmMap` expands them into one character per tile. The homestead sits
    at tile (56, 62): the estate gives it 48 x 32 tiles, the homestead map
    is 48 x 30, so the two rows above its north hedge are the great fields'
    thicket. A 4-tile lane is cut through the woods below the south gate.
  - Hedges, the house, the workshop, the coop, the chest, the pond, the
    bench, the apple tree, the lamp, the rock, the mailbox, the woods and
    every parcel's thicket are solid. The pen is walkable (its fence is
    down).
  - Only the first field (48 tiles) can be tilled; the old fields wait for
    the Clear spell (`base.md`, "Built later", step 3).
- **The farm scene** (`scenes/farm/farm.tscn`) replaces the farm test
  scene: the follow camera (ADR-0015), the farming HUD, F8/F9/F10 as
  before. No combat is loaded (ADR-0016). `make_test_farm()` stays for the
  farming rule tests and the two farming review sheets.
- **Drawing:** the farm's ground is painted once into one texture
  (`FarmGround`) and tinted by the time of day when drawn; soil and crops
  on screen, the buildings and things, the player (in order of their base
  line), the window lights and the porch lamp are drawn each frame.
- **One game across places** (`GameState`): one clock, one bag, one Mana.
  Each place builds its simulation from the game when it opens and stores
  what it remembers when the player leaves.
  - The farm remembers its soil and crops, rain, the selected spell and
    tier. Days that passed while away run on return: crops watered before
    leaving grow once, however many days passed.
  - Millbrook remembers notes found, the crank and other things taken, the
    sluice, the drain, Grist defeated and where its heart lies, and the
    creatures defeated today (back at the next 6:00, even across a visit).
- **The gate** (ADR-0016): walking out of the farm's lane arrives at the
  top of Millbrook's lane facing into Millbrook; walking up Millbrook's lane
  arrives in the farm's lane below the gate, facing the homestead. A 0.3 s
  fade through the time of day's dark (`Fade`).
- **Falling** (Q36): the next 6:00 at the farm, in front of the farmhouse
  door, with Mana full and everything carried kept.
- **Review scene:** `farm_dusk` (19:00, the field part sown, the lane).

## Left out, and why

- **The farmhouse inside and the bed.** `base.md` builds the inside as step
  2. Until then the door is the bed, as on the test field, and the player
  wakes in front of it, not in bed with the cat on the blanket (Q36). The
  cat is step 4.
- **The mailbox, bench and chest do nothing yet**; the Clear spell, the
  parcels opening, the pen's animals and the workshop come later
  (`base.md`, "Built later").
- **The old track** under the great fields' thicket is 2 tiles west of the
  north gate in the estate map (it runs down estate column 17, tiles
  68-71; the gate is at tiles 70-73). It is under thicket, so nothing
  shows; it needs lining up when the great fields open.

## What broke, and how it was found

- **The first render** showed the hedge, the thicket and the woods in
  near-identical dark greens, and the bench and the rock painted four times
  each (they cover 2 x 2 tiles and were painted per tile). The hedge is now
  a tended green with a lit top and a shadowed foot, the thicket a cool
  green with pale canes, the woods the darkest; the bench and rock are
  drawn once as things.
- **The second render** showed the woods' crowns on a visible grid; they
  now vary in size and spill over tile edges.
- **A deliberate break survived:** when an area is rebuilt, a creature
  defeated today was taken off its roster entry but left standing in the
  simulation, and the test only looked at the roster. The test now counts
  the creatures in the area.
