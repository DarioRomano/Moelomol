# 2026-10-11: Rot seed (Q31 A)

**Asked (the lead):** "Q31 let's go with the rot seed. Does siphoning stacks
do damage?"

## Done

- **Rot seed on magic's skill**, replacing the interim Rot pool (values in
  `docs/design/combat.md`):
  - a slow seed (160 px/s, 15 Focus) that does no damage and latches onto
    the first creature it hits;
  - it grows Rot there: 1 at once, 1 more every 0.6 s, for 4 s in all;
  - when its host dies, or a Siphon pull drains it, it jumps to the
    nearest other creature within 48 px, keeping its time; with none in
    reach it is lost;
  - one seed at a time, a new one replaces it.
- Drawn: the seed in flight, a growth on the host that lengthens as it
  ages and fades in its last second, a ring as it takes hold, and an arc
  when it jumps. The HUD shows the seed's time left. Haptics: the seed's
  landing and jump use the small spell-hit tick.
- The Rot pool and its payload are removed (the Chill pool behind Wardstep
  stays).
- Q31 marked resolved.
- **The question, answered and recorded as a reading in `combat.md`:**
  draining does no damage. The tether only moves stacks into the lantern;
  the damage is all in the beam. Drained stacks also stop burning on the
  creature, so a long Siphon trades ticking damage for a bigger burst.
- The art prompts (`combat-art-prompts.md`) were still describing Release,
  the Rot pool, the marker arrow and the heavy arrow; updated for this and
  yesterday's round: the guard and perfect-guard effects, the roll for
  every weapon, the Volley shot, the seed, its growth and jump, the
  Siphon's poses, tether and beam.

## What broke, and how it was found

- **The first version of the wither test failed:** the seed loses a tick on
  the tick it latches, and the test helper steps a variable number of
  ticks. The test now measures from the time left.
- **A deliberate break survived:** making the jump take the last creature
  in reach instead of the nearest passed, because only one creature was in
  reach. The test now has two, nearer listed first; the break fails it.
- I had written a meaningless assertion (health compared against a count of
  a nonexistent event) in the first seed test; replaced with a plain check
  that the seed does no damage, before committing.
