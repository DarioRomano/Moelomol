# 2026-11-06: The cat, a cat of places (Q41 A)

`base.md`'s build step 4. The lead chose Q41 A on 2026-11-06.

## Done

- **`Cat`** (`src/farm/cat.gd`), held by the game (`GameState.cat`) and
  saved (`"cat"` in the save; older saves start it in the basket).
  - **Its places** (`base.md`): the basket by the stove, the window seat,
    the porch step, the old apple tree, the pond bench and the workshop
    roof; and the blanket, only after a fall.
  - **Its day:** the basket 6:00 to 8:00; the porch step (even days) or
    the apple tree (odd days) until noon; the apple tree (even) or the
    workshop roof (odd) until 17:00; the bench until 20:30, in the sunset;
    the window seat through the night.
  - **It moves only unseen:** to its next place only when the player can
    see neither the place it leaves nor the place it goes to. At the farm
    "seen" is the camera's view (grown by 24 px); in the house the whole
    room is in view, so it never comes or goes while the player is inside.
    It is never seen walking: it is simply there.
  - **Stroking:** Interact (F / B) while facing it. A long low purr on the
    controller (`cat_purr`), its eyes close for 1.5 s. No text.
  - **After a fall** it is curled on the blanket when the player wakes
    (Q36), and stays while the player is inside.
- Drawing (`CatDrawer`, placeholder): a small grey cat sitting, its tail
  round its feet, two warm points for eyes that stay untinted at night;
  curled asleep on the blanket.
- Review scenes: `farm_cat_bench` (the bench at dusk); `house_night` now
  has it on the window seat.

## Left out, and why

- The impossible moments (`story.md`) come with the story's stages (Q41).
- Feeding it treats waits for cooking (not designed).
- The cat is not solid: it sits where the player walks (the porch step),
  and blocking the doorway would trap the player.
- At the bed, the cat is checked before the bed: with the cat on the
  blanket, facing it strokes it rather than sleeping. The player wakes
  beside the bed facing away, so this needs a deliberate turn.

## What broke, and how it was found

- The first bench render had the player standing on the bench, hiding it
  and the cat (the review scene placed the player without checking the
  tiles); moved beside it. A zoomed crop of the render showed the cat reads
  as a cat at 3x.
- Deliberate breaks, all caught: moving while watched; moving while its
  next place is watched; no blanket after a fall; the cat not saved;
  stroking never happening; one place all morning; the farm never seeing
  it; unknown places loaded from a save.
