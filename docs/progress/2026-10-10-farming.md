## What broke and how it was found

- **The first renders showed four problems:**
  - **The path read as water.** Its grey-blue stone turned bluer under the
    dawn tint. It is now pale worn earth (`WILD[3]`).
  - **The windows' light pool drew as grey discs.** It is now many faint,
    flattened amber rings, which fade at the edge.
  - **Night was too dark to read.** At a tint of 0.32, dry and watered soil
    looked the same, against the design's own "readable at a glance" aim.
    The night tint was raised. The clock test that encoded the old darkness
    now checks luminance and blueness instead.
  - **The player overlaps the house front at the door.** That is right for
    the ¾ view (ADR-0014), so it was left as is.
- **The review sheet's own setup was wrong at first.** It grew each cell
  with `new_day()`, which grows and dries every tile, so cells set up
  earlier would have grown again. It now plants everything first, then
  advances the days together.
- **The scene called a private method to sleep.** `FarmSim.sleep()` is now
  public.
- **`seed` is a built-in GDScript function**, so the crop field is
  `seed_item`.
- **All 28 rule tests passed on the first run.** That is why the deliberate
  breaks mattered:
  - 26 breaks in all, one per rule: pace, cap at 2:00, phase edges,
    wrapping past midnight, smooth tint, lights ramp, tilling blocked
    ground, water only on tilled soil, no double planting, growth only when
    watered, soil drying, stopping when ripe, seeds back, seed use, the
    empty-pouch check, standing still while working, waking at home,
    growth on sleep, the solid house, tool wrap, the path, two rumble
    links, the F2 entry, the interact binding.
  - Every one fails a test except one: the clamp in `CropKind.stage_for`.
    Integer division already keeps the stage below "ripe", so breaking the
    clamp changes nothing.
  - The scene-level breaks needed `test_farm_scene.gd` first; without it,
    nothing tested the bindings, F2, or the scene playing rumble.

## Not done (and why)

- **The decisions:** Q21–Q27 are the lead's; everything built follows
  option A and is cheap to change.
- **Saving:** the farm resets on quit. Saving is its own design and
  architecture decision, likely an ADR.
- **Left for later:**
  - the pet at the farm, the cat treats recipe, crafting;
  - farming upgrades (can capacity, multi-tile hoe, sprinklers);
  - clearing crops with a confirmation;
  - animals, seasons and weather;
  - pausing in menus (there are none yet).
  None was asked for in this step.
- **Real 2D lights:** the warm window light is drawn untinted over the
  tinted world. Real lights wait for the base art and a measurement against
  ADR-0007.
- **Not checkable here:** the pace, the light's mood, feel, rumble and
  real-machine performance. They are on the playtest checklist.
