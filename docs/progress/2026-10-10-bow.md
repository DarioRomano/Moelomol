# 2026-10-10: Bow

**Asked:** the other weapons, after Q20 = A; this is the second of three
PRs (hammer #19, bow, magic). Stacked on #19 (it needs the `Weapon` classes
and the charge state).

## Done

- **Arrows** (`src/combat/projectile.gd`): straight flight at 360 px/s for
  up to 260 px. Each tick an arrow hits every creature whose circle its path
  crosses, nearest first, and stops at the first unless it pierces, or at
  walls and pillars. A `CombatMove` can carry an `arrow` that is fired when
  its active part starts; `melee = false` makes the move itself hit nothing.
  Soft aim reaches the arrow's range for shots.
- **Zones** (`src/combat/zone.gd`): areas that hit in timed waves (the
  Volley rain; magic's pools will use them).
- **Bow** (`src/combat/bow.gd`):
  - Quick shot.
  - Three-stage draw: strong, piercing, heavy with knockback. 60% walking
    speed while drawing.
  - Clean release (+25%), and a stamina drain while holding stage 3, with a
    forced release when stamina runs out.
  - Dodge shot (heavy during a roll looses a piercing arrow as the roll
    ends).
  - Flow: max 5, 8% faster draw per stack, lost on any hit.
  - Volley: a marker, then a rain sized by the Flow spent.
  - Values and the readings taken of the design are in
    `docs/design/combat.md`, "Bow as built".
- **Weapon hooks:**
  - `charge_move_speed`, `dodge_action` / `on_dodge_end`, `on_hit_landed`,
    `on_projectile_stopped`, `tick`.
  - `on_owner_hit` now reaches every weapon in the loadout, not only the
    one in hand: getting hit costs Rhythm and Flow either way.
- **Loadout key:** F7 in the arena changes the weapon not in hand (cycling
  greatsword, hammer, bow, never doubling the one in hand). The default
  loadout stays greatsword + hammer.
- **Drawing:** placeholder bow (carried, drawn with the string pulled per
  stage, raised for Volley), draw pips, arrows (piercing ones longer and
  amber, heavy ones thicker, the marker with a red fletch), the mark, the
  rain zone with falling arrows.
- **Rumble:** stage tick, release snap, arrow hit, heavy-arrow hit. There is
  no rumble per rain hit: many small hits at once would only blur.
- **Renders:** `bow_poses.tscn` (stage 2 draw, piercing line, heavy
  knockback, dodge shot, the mark, rain at Flow 3), added to
  `tools/render-showcase.sh`.
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/test_combat_bow.gd` (28 tests), plus the F7 test in
  `test_combat_arena.gd`. Suite: 169 passing.
- **Playtest checklist:** `docs/playtests/2026-10-10-bow.md`.

## What broke and how it was found

- **The heavy-arrow knockback test failed** at first: the creature had
  moved only 6 px. The test stopped stepping as soon as the player was free,
  while the 0.2 s push was still playing out. The rule was right; the test
  now lets the push finish.
- **Deliberate breaks** (20, in three rounds) found two untested rules,
  each now covered:
  - Removing the guard that stops a shot from hitting in melee changed
    nothing in the tests. New test: a creature touching the player straight
    ahead is hit only by the arrow.
  - Nothing tested how long a rain zone lasts, or that a lingering zone
    never fires a fourth wave. New zone test.
  - Two first-round results were my own errors: a filter that matched no
    test by name, and a break pattern with the wrong indentation. Both were
    rerun correctly; every break now makes a test fail.
- **The arena help line ran off a 4:3 screen** once "F7 other weapon" was
  added (1024×768 render). It is now two lines.
